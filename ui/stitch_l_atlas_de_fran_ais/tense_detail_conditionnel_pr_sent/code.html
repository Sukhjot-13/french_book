Conditionnel Présent

Would / Could / Should

psychology HYPOTHETICAL

The present conditional is used to express events that are not guaranteed to occur, often dependent on certain conditions. It is the French equivalent of "would" + verb, used heavily in polite requests, hypotheses, and future actions viewed from a past perspective.

build Formation

The conditional is formed using the future stem (typically the infinitive) combined with the imparfait endings.

Future Stem + -ais, -ais, -ait, -ions, -iez, -aient

REGULAR ENDINGS (PARLER)

  • je parlerais
  • tu parlerais
  • il/elle parlerait
  • nous parlerions
  • vous parleriez
  • ils/elles parleraient

COMMON IRREGULAR STEMS

  • aller ir- (irais)
  • avoir aur- (aurais)
  • être ser- (serais)
  • faire fer- (ferais)
  • savoir saur- (saurais)
  • venir viendr- (viendrais)

rule Usage Rules

1. Politeness & Softened Requests

To express a wish, a request, or advice politely, avoiding the harshness of the present or imperative.

Je voudrais un café, s'il vous plaît.

2. Hypotheses (Si Clauses)

Used in the main clause when expressing a hypothetical situation that is contrary to current facts (Si + Imparfait ➔ Conditionnel).

Si j'avais de l'argent, j'achèterais une voiture.

3. Future in the Past

Used in indirect speech when the main reporting verb is in a past tense, and the reported action was future relative to that past moment.

Il a dit qu'il viendrait demain.
