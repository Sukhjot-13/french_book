Common Mistake

Beaucoup des vs. Beaucoup de

Quantities are always followed by 'de' (or d') without the definite article, unless referring to a specific, previously mentioned group.

close Incorrect

J'ai beaucoup des amis en France.

check Correct

J'ai beaucoup d'amis en France.

Preposition Trap

Manquer à vs. Manquer de

The verb 'manquer' changes meaning completely depending on the preposition used, and the subject/object relationship is inverted with 'manquer à'.

Tu me manques.

Meaning: I miss you. (Literally: You are missing to me)

Il manque de courage.

Meaning: He lacks courage.
