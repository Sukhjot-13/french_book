prendre

Irregular Auxiliary: Avoir Essential Very Common Ch. 4, 7, 9

To take, to catch (transport), to have (food/drink). A highly versatile and frequent irregular verb belonging to the 3rd group.

overview Overview

Core Meaning

The primary translation is "to take" (physically grasping something or taking a mode of transport). It is also heavily used for consuming food or drink.

Usage Note

Unlike in English, one does not "make" a decision in French; one "takes" it (prendre une décision). Do not confuse with apporter or emmener.
