==================================================
EXTRACTION RULES
==================================================
- Independent ground-zero extraction only; use only supplied verb-table pages/text.
- Do not compare against the existing master dataset.
- First identify and store each table's printed column_order.
- Never infer column meaning from position without checking the table header.
- CRITICAL: in a layout like:
  infinitive | past participle | je | tu | il/elle/on | nous | vous | ils/elles
  the past participle is NOT the je form.
- Preserve exactly printed forms as source_type = book.
- Do not generate additional conjugations from general French knowledge.
- If only present indicative is printed, populate only present_indicative.
- Keep participles separate from finite person forms.
- Preserve accents and apostrophes exactly.
- Do not infer auxiliary or English meaning unless supplied.
- Put broken/ambiguous rows in uncertain_rows.
- Remove headers, footers, page numbers, and layout artifacts.
- Do not silently lose rows.
- Keep exact page/table provenance.
- Every legitimate row must be accounted for as emitted, duplicate-collapsed, uncertain, or rejected non-data artifact.
