EXTRACTION RULES
==================================================
- Independent ground-zero extraction only; use only supplied glossary pages/text.
- Do not compare against the existing master dataset.
- Preserve direction exactly: fr_en or en_fr.
- Keep source_headword and source_target separate.
- Never merge English and French columns into one string.
- Never produce malformed rows such as "to have a cold avoir un rhume", "time de temps en temps", or "you merci".
- Preserve parenthetical grammatical variants correctly.
- Do not classify everything as a noun.
- Recognize verbs, adjectives, adverbs, phrases, idioms, collocations, and verb patterns when source-supported.
- For English infinitives such as "accept, to" or "to accept", pair cleanly with French "accepter".
- If a row is ambiguous, put it in uncertain_rows rather than guessing.
- Remove headers, footers, page numbers, copyright text, and cross-column fragments.
- Do not silently drop legitimate source rows.
- Keep exact page/column provenance.
- Do not invent meanings, variants, or POS from general knowledge.
- Every legitimate source row must be accounted for as emitted, duplicate-collapsed, uncertain, or rejected non-data artifact.
